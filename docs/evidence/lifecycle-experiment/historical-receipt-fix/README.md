## Executed checks

- Twelve people receive durable assignments: six treatment and six holdout. Two treatment people are eligible; three holdouts would otherwise qualify. All holdout dispatches are suppressed.
- One lost acknowledgment and one child exit 86 recover exactly two independent action IDs with matching payload hashes. Replays create no additional effect. Unresolved acceptance is retained separately until readback.
- Four source-qualified conversions become three after a late correction. An explicit synthetic USD 125,000 opportunity loses its exact conversion link; an unmapped USD 9,000 opportunity never contributes. Neither is revenue or causal lifecycle value.
- The 1,200-person scenario has 627 treatment and 573 holdout assignments and 399 corrected conversions. No HTTP effects are executed at that scale. The complete scenario digest and reference metrics match the first executed run.
- Nine dbt models and 13 data tests execute. Corrected incremental and independent full-refresh rowsets agree; Python independently reconciles counts, receipt exposure and exact-linked pipeline.
- A real-HTTP late-receipt regression confirms that reconciliation after the cutoff cannot change an earlier report. Historical integrity checks use visible acceptance history rather than today's mutable action state.

**443 local tests passed** (424 preserved baseline + 19 experiment cases), with zero failures, errors or skips. Pending/uncovered outcome rows display **Unknown** rather than a known non-conversion.
